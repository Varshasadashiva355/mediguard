ℹ️ MediGuard AI – Clinical Architecture & Regulatory Disclaimers

⚠️ Non-Negotiable Research Prototype Notice

MediGuard AI is an experimental research and clinical decision-support prototype. It is NOT an FDA/CE-cleared or clinically validated diagnostic product. It must never be used as an autonomous diagnostic device on human patients without regulatory clearance.

Core Design & Ethical Directives

  • Physician Ownership (Doctors Own 100%): Every diagnosis, treatment order, catheterization decision, and discharge is the sole responsibility of licensed cardiologists. AI predictions are supplementary experimental signals.
  • Uncalibrated Model Scores: Output scores are raw continuous sigmoid activations ($0.00$ to $1.00$) trained on the PTB-XL database. They are experimental indications, not calibrated statistical probabilities of heart attacks.
  • Separate Image/PDF vs Waveform Pipelines (Rule 4): Scanned images and clinical PDFs follow strict non-ML document archival pipelines. We never fabricate model scores or use unvalidated OCR for diagnostic interpretation.
  • Zero Patient Data Exfiltration (Rule 5): Operates strictly on synthetic cardiology profiles in a private hospital-hosted environment. Patient data is never dispatched to external public AI cloud APIs.
  • Emergency Protocol Priority (Rule 9): Acute coronary symptoms (STEMI, syncope, shock) trigger immediate hospital emergency hotlines (Ext. 2222 / 4400) and must never wait for background AI processing.
Model Technical Specification:
Architecture: ResNet-1D (12-Lead Temporal Convolutions) • Checkpoint: PTB-XL Benchmark • Classes: NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP • Signal Sampling: 100/500 Hz standard digital waveforms.
